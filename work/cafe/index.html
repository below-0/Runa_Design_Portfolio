Our story

Started with one good coffee machine and a lot of stubbornness.

Birchwood opened as a small counter with a handful of tables — the idea was simple: proper coffee, honest baking, and a place people actually want to sit for a while.

Everything's still made fresh on site, the same way it was on day one.
