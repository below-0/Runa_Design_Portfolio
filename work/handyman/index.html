Questions

What people usually ask.

What kind of jobs do you take on?

Pretty much anything short of a full renovation — furniture assembly, shelving, minor repairs, painting touch-ups, and general odd jobs around the house.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free and no-obligation — you'll know the cost before any work starts.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured for work in and around your home.

How quickly can you come out?

Most small jobs can be booked within a few days — sooner if it's urgent, just ask.

Is there a minimum job size?

No — genuinely no job too small. A single shelf or a five-minute fix is fine.
